# Code of conduct

## Commitment

We want participation in this project to be respectful, accessible, and technically rigorous. People are welcome regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality, appearance, race, religion, sexual orientation, or level of experience.

This is a project-authored policy. It does not claim adoption or certification by an external organization.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss evidence and code without attacking people.
- Explain disagreements clearly and accept good-faith corrections.
- Credit contributions and make review feedback actionable.
- Respect language differences, accessibility needs, and reasonable boundaries.
- Report methodological mistakes honestly; do not pressure contributors to make results flattering.
- Use synthetic examples and protect private information during discussion.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, hateful or discriminatory remarks, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, impersonation, retaliation, repeated disruption, and publishing private information without authorization are unacceptable.

Knowingly fabricating evidence, misrepresenting review or independence, or exposing protected evaluation material is also unacceptable. Ordinary technical errors and good-faith criticism are not misconduct.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository discussions, issues, PRs, reviews, project communication channels, and public representation of the project. It applies while the repository is private and after publication.

## Reporting and privacy

Contact a repository maintainer through an existing private channel. Do not put private reports or protected data into public issues. Before publication, maintainers must establish a private conduct-reporting channel, name the responsible maintainers, and provide an alternate recipient for reports involving a maintainer.

Provide the minimum information needed to investigate. Reports are shared only as necessary for investigation and response; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised. No retaliation against good-faith reporters is permitted.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may request a correction, remove content, pause a discussion, warn a participant, temporarily restrict participation, or permanently exclude someone for serious or repeated misconduct. Consider severity, context, recurrence, and safety; explain decisions privately where appropriate.

A maintainer with a conflict of interest must recuse themselves where another responsible maintainer is available. Participants may request reconsideration through an uninvolved maintainer. Governance and reporting contacts must be completed before public community participation opens.
